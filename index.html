How we build

A few rules we do not break

Idle means idle

No polling, no background chatter. An app that has nothing to do uses nothing.

Native, not wrapped

Real Windows apps that follow your theme, your DPI and your notification area.

Nothing leaves your machine

No accounts, no analytics. Keys you enter are encrypted for your Windows user alone.
